Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy says what Dazbee collects when you use it, why, and what you can do about it. Dazbee is run from India; you can reach us at dazbee.ai@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in, with Google or with your email, we receive your name and email address, and from Google your profile picture. Sign-in is handled for us by WorkOS.

Your work. The designs, brand kits, images, fonts and other files you and your team make or upload, kept so you can open and edit them.

How you use it. Basic records our servers keep to run the service and keep it secure, such as when you signed in.

What we use it for

To sign you in, to show you and your team your workspaces and work, to send you invites and sign-in emails, and to keep the service working and secure. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Dazbee asks only for your email address and basic profile (name and picture). We use them only to sign you in and to show who you are to your team. We don't share them, except with the services listed below that run Dazbee for us. Dazbee's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who else handles it

Services that run parts of Dazbee for us: WorkOS for sign-in, and our hosting and storage providers. Each handles your information only to provide that service to us.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account or your team's workspace exists. You can delete your account yourself, in Dazbee's Settings. Deleting it removes your information at once: your name and email, your sign-in, and every workspace you own alone, with all the designs, brand kits, images, fonts and other files in it. What you made in a workspace you share with a team stays with that team. If you own a workspace with other members, you hand it over to one of them first.

We keep a nightly backup of our database for 14 days, so we can recover from a fault. A deleted account leaves the backups within 14 days, as the older copies are deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by writing to dazbee.ai@gmail.com. You can delete your account at any time in Dazbee's Settings, or ask us to by writing to the same address. You can remove Dazbee's access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account's security settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
